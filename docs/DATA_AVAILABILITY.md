# Data availability and redistribution boundaries

## Public source records

Dataset accessions, DOI records, genome builds, sample descriptors, and their role in the analysis are recorded in `metadata/dataset_accessions.tsv`. These identifiers point users to the original providers; the repository does not mirror their data.

## Data not redistributed here

The following are intentionally absent:

- Swedish and HUNT microbiome GWAS summary statistics;
- disease GWAS summary statistics;
- 1000 Genomes and other LD-reference files;
- PLINK genotype/reference bundles;
- individual-level genotype, phenotype, sequencing, or microbiome data;
- CATlas, Bryois, CEDAR-2, and other third-party archives whose redistribution rights were not confirmed for this release;
- controlled-access data and credentials.

Users must download or apply for these materials through the listed original records and follow their terms. A publicly reachable record is not, by itself, permission to redistribute its files.

## Study-generated derived outputs

This staging package contains small aggregate tables and figure source data generated by the study. They do not contain individual-level records. Their public licence remains pending author confirmation; consequently they must not be pushed or archived as a formal release yet.

## Known metadata limitations

Per-variant microbial GWAS sample sizes, INFO values, and study-specific MAF were not uniformly available. Regional sensitivity analyses used cohort-level sample sizes and 1000 Genomes European reference MAF where documented. Disease datasets also differ in metadata completeness. These limitations are preserved rather than imputed.
